NEED A LITTLE CLARITY?

Good questions
deserve good answers.

We want you to feel confident about every step. Here are a few things members often ask.

Contact our team ↗
Is RishtaSutra free to join?+

This demo shows a free plan and optional paid memberships. Actual plans and paid features must be enabled through a secure backend before launch.

Who can see my contact details?+

Contact details should remain private by default. A production service should only share them with your explicit consent and according to your privacy settings.

Are the profiles on this page real?+

No. All profiles on this demonstration website are fictional sample data and are labelled for demonstration. They do not represent actual members.

How do I report or block a profile?+

Use the report or block control on a profile card. This front-end demo stores the action only in your current browser; a live service needs server-side review and enforcement.

Does a paid plan guarantee a match?+

No. Membership can provide additional tools, but it cannot guarantee compatibility, a response, an engagement or marriage.
